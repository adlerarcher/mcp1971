Prototype. Not a DOE or agency product. Captured 20 August 2026. Lease and well records are simulated. Not for permitting decisions.

Responses were produced by four Model Context Protocol servers: ecfr (eCFR and Federal Register APIs), ipac (USFWS IPaC Location API), sepi-stub (simulated BLM MLRS and AFMSS response shapes), and gpcp-timeline (deterministic critical-path computation over an encoded dependency rule set). Sequence and duration logic is computed from the rule set, not generated by a language model. Rule set v0.2.0.
