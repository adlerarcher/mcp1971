Across MLRS geothermal lease records, case names are blank or null, and expiration dates include values in the year 3000. Fifty years of leasing at this location shows 41 closed cases and 32 currently authorized, none producing.

Each source names the same geothermal location with a different identifier. The project polygon is the only key that currently joins those names. Joins that would connect a lease to a NEPA docket or a well record do not resolve.
